About Us

We’re Tara & Mattel—two introverts in our mid-40s doing life, parenting, and remote work together. With Tara’s background in finance and Mattel’s experience in digital marketing and design, we’re teaming up to share what we’ve learned about careers, money, family, and finding true ourselves. Why are we doing this? Because we know what it's like to feel lost and search for answers all by yourself. Now, it’s our turn to give back, bringing you honest stories, practical tools, and baby steps you can actually take—so you never have to feel like you’re figuring it all out alone.

For Foundations & NGOs

Akunting Nirlaba ISAK 335

For you who manage the finances of a foundation or NGO

Running an NGO is ultimately about honoring trust. Especially when our work relies on grants, transparent fund management is crucial to ensure our programs bring the maximum benefit to the community. The Akunting Nirlaba ISAK 335 system is a web-based solution that finally ends the headache of messy, layered spreadsheets by automatically separating funds and organizing reports neatly. Let this tool handle the accounting so you can get back to what matters most: executing programs and making a real impact on the ground!
